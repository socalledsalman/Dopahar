# **Dopahar**

A minimal, 100% offline, ad-free daily planner for Android. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud lock-in—all your data stays locally on your device.

---

## **Features**

* **Today Dashboard** — Quick-add strip, daily stats, 7-day snapshot, Plan Tomorrow queue, and a 3-line daily reflection with mood tracking.
* **Habits** — 7-day and 14-day tracking grids, 30-day heatmaps, and Rest Day (freeze) tokens to protect streaks.
* **Schedule & Routines** — Hourly time-blocking timeline, reusable daily routines, offline reminders, and `.ics` calendar export.
* **To-Do List** — Priority levels, swipe-to-complete/reschedule gestures, and automatic rollover for unfinished tasks.
* **Budget & IOUs** — Monthly spend limits, category breakdowns, search/filters, and a dedicated Lent/Borrowed (IOU) tracker.
* **Focus Detox** — Focus timer and stopwatch with Android Screen Pinning, plus one-tap task/block linking.
* **Privacy & Security** — 4-digit PIN lock with native Android Fingerprint unlock.
* **Personalization** — AMOLED, Dark, and Light surfaces, 5 bundled offline fonts, and a custom accent color wheel.
* **Backup & Restore** — Export/share local JSON backups anytime, or connect optional Google Drive auto-sync.

---

## **Download & Install**

1. Open the [**Releases**](https://github.com/socalledsalman/Dopahar/releases) page.
2. Download the latest **`Dopahar-v1.0.apk`** under **Assets**.
3. Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
